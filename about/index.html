About the Recognition Program

Celebrating Our People

Recognizing the people who make a difference every day.

Our Purpose

The Employee of the Quarter program is designed to recognize employees who demonstrate dedication, excellence, teamwork, and commitment in their daily work.

Why Recognition Matters

Every employee contributes to the quality of care and service we provide. Recognition gives us an opportunity to celebrate those contributions and appreciate the people behind our work.

What We Celebrate

We celebrate outstanding performance, compassion, teamwork, responsibility, initiative, and the positive impact employees make on their colleagues and the people we serve.

Our Goal

Our goal is to build a culture where great work is noticed, appreciated, and shared. Recognition helps create a workplace where employees feel valued and motivated to make a difference.

Every Contribution Matters

Together, we recognize the dedication, care, and excellence that make a difference every day.
