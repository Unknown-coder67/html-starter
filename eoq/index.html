First: Professional and Behavioral Commitment

  1. Adherence to working hours, attendance, and departure times.
  2. Compliance with hospital rules and policies.
  3. Adherence to professional ethics and medical confidentiality.
  4. Professional appearance and adherence to the official uniform/dress code.
  5. Respect for supervisors, colleagues, patients, and visitors.

Second: Quality of Job Performance

  1. Proficiency in performing assigned job duties.
  2. Accuracy in carrying out tasks and minimizing errors.
  3. Timely completion of transactions and tasks without compromising quality.
  4. Ability to work effectively under pressure.

Third: Patient and Visitor Relations

  1. Courteous reception and positive communication.
  2. Commitment to patient service and satisfaction.
  3. Professional and tactful handling of complaints and difficult situations.
  4. Demonstrating empathy and consideration for patients’ human needs.
  5. Maintaining patient dignity and privacy.

Fourth: Teamwork and Cooperation

  1. Cooperation with team members.
  2. Contributing to a positive work environment.
  3. Sharing knowledge and experience with colleagues.
  4. Supporting other departments when needed.

Fifth: Discipline and Safety

  1. Compliance with occupational health and safety procedures.
  2. Compliance with infection prevention and control policies (for clinical staff).
  3. Reporting hazards or professional errors.
  4. Proper use of equipment and tools.
  5. Proper care and protection of hospital property.

Sixth: Professional Development and Learning

  1. Commitment to professional self-development.
  2. Participation in training courses and workshops.
  3. Applying acquired knowledge and skills in the workplace.
  4. Keeping up to date with new instructions, policies, and guidelines.
  5. Demonstrating a willingness to improve performance.

Seventh: Initiative and Innovation

  1. Suggesting ideas to improve work processes.
  2. Effectively identifying and solving problems.
  3. Taking initiative without waiting for direction.
  4. Participating in quality improvement projects.
  5. Demonstrating flexibility and adaptability to change.

Eighth: Responsibility and Reliability

  1. Taking responsibility for assigned duties.
  2. Meeting deadlines.
  3. Demonstrating professional honesty and integrity.
  4. Maintaining the confidentiality and security of patient and hospital information.
